Hotel Policies

🚭 Smoking Policy
ANEMOS is a 100% non-smoking resort inside guest rooms and indoor public spaces. Designated smoking areas are available near the beach garden pavilion.
🕑 Quiet Hours
To guarantee every guest a restful sleep, quiet hours are observed between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM. Please keep balcony and television volumes moderate.
🏊 Pool Guidelines
The infinity pool operates from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Showering is required prior to entering. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.
💳 Payment & Incidental Deposit
All major credit/debit cards, UPI, and cash are accepted. Room charges are settled upon check-out.
